Research

Research

Research Focus
Multimodal Transformer Architectures for Cross-Modal Fusion and Predictive Analysis in Complex Vision Systems

Traditional crop monitoring relies on single-modality visual cues which frequently fail to detect diseases in their latent stages. This research leverages Multimodal Transformers to fuse high-resolution imagery, genomic sequence data, hyperspectral data, and environmental sensor metadata — capturing complex temporal and spatial dependencies for both latent disease detection and survival analysis.

🧬

Multimodal Data Fusion

Fusing RGB imagery, genomic sequences, hyperspectral data, and environmental sensors using transformer-based architectures.

🔬

Latent Disease Detection

Detecting crop diseases before visible symptoms appear using genomic markers and physiological stress indicators.

📈

Survival Analysis

Predicting long-term crop survival probability under environmental stressors using temporal multimodal models.

🌾

Precision Breeding

Supporting precision breeding programmes with AI-driven early warnings for disease-prone genotypes.
