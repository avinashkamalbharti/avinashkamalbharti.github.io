About

About Me

I am a PhD scholar in Computer Science and Engineering at IIT Indore. I design transformer models that fuse RNA-seq gene expression with images: pathway-level gene tokens query image patches through cross-attention, so the biological prior shapes what the model attends to. I also work on rigorous evaluation of multimodal models. My research direction is human biomedical AI: integrating molecular profiles with imaging and clinical data, and foundation models for single-cell and multi-omics analysis. Before the PhD, I completed an M.Tech with a thesis on machine learning for diabetes risk prediction, worked as a software developer, and have taught computer science for seven years.

Research

Research

Research Focus
Pathway-guided multimodal transformers for gene expression and imaging

My doctoral project fuses RNA-seq profiles with images to detect plant disease before visible symptoms appear. Pathway-level gene tokens query image patches through cross-attention, aligned with a CLIP-style contrastive objective; the evaluation design includes a reversed-attention ablation and unimodal, concatenation and co-attention baselines. The same design carries over to human biomedical data, which is where my research is heading.

🧬

Multimodal Fusion

Cross-attention between pathway-level gene tokens and image patches, with contrastive alignment of modalities that have no ground-truth pairing.

🔬

Testable Interpretability

Attention maps that show where a pathway's signal appears in an image, checked through ablations rather than assumed.

📈

Survival Analysis

Survival models that combine gene expression and whole-slide images, evaluated with the C-index on public cancer cohorts.

✅

Rigorous Evaluation

Checking how much reported gains hold up across random seeds, data splits and model-selection choices.
